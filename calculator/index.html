    Formula (v0.8, Section 3.4): AIVSS = (CVSS_Base + AARS) × Mitigation_Factor, where
    AARS = (10 − CVSS_Base) × (Factor_Sum / 10) × ThM, Factor_Sum = sum of the 10 factors below
    (max 10.0), rounded to the nearest tenth (round-half-up). This is a different, complementary
    methodology from the SSVC decision-tree tool at aivss.owasp.org/ssvc.html — the two are not
    meant to produce comparable outputs.
